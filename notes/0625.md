## red herrig

it was a red herring. i actually debuged and found: 149,650 religions created over the run — 10,801 still active at the end, 138,849 extinct. That's ~1,000 religions
born and dying per generation.

4kish binomial calls per religion, it gets messy quick. even tho I skip extinct religions that's still alot, and 1000 per tick is definitely broken

- the issue: when ppl schism and convert to new religion, i dump them into the same heterodoxy band from old religion into the new one and since converts have to be heterodox to move anyway, then this new religion immeidatley hits schism territory, breaks and forms aonther etc and they all go extinct mmediatley. instead, we should reset their het so when they enter new religion, they become the orthodoxy.

- but should be scaled based on new religion. in fact this will help w/ conversion modelling which i want to add later, where ppl can convert between existing religions as well not just in schism

## 2 changes:

- dead religions will be in a vec of dead religions now taht we push to as they die, consumed from active religion slotmap. that way we only ever walk the active ones, and ignore dead ones. but we can keep the dead ones for the end for readout

- converts will get scaled by their mean (as the daughter religion mean) so that they don't immeidatley scism again
