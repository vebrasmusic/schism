--- perf diagnostics
using flamegraph on the new release run instead of non-release, narrowed down the hot paths:

```


What the stack is doing
Everything funnels through Simulation::run, which splits into three real workloads:
1. Adherent::try_birth — 39% (3,043 running, 720 self). This is your hot path by a mile. But notice the Self is only 720 — try_birth itself isn't the problem, its callee is. Almost all of it is:
2. Beta::sample — 29% (2,255 running, 1,050 self). This is rand_distr sampling from a Beta distribution, and it's the single biggest concrete cost in the whole program. Half its time is in its own code, half in children (those 0x13dc / 0x1494 unsymbolicated frames are almost certainly the underlying gamma-variate sampling and the RNG — Beta is generated from two Gamma draws under the hood).
3. build_generation_readout — 18% (1,405 running, 484 self). Reporting/aggregation. The expensive child here is BuildHasher::hash_one — 7.6% (594 running, 397 self) — sitting on top of <core::hash::si..., i.e. SipHasher, the default std::HashMap hasher. And hashing shows up again in that left tower over try_birth (the BuildHasher::has... + alloc::vec boxes), so hashing is a diffuse cost spread across the program, not just in the readout.
```
